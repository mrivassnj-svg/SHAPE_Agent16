# SHAPE Agent 16 — Evidence Guidelines

**Project:** SHAPE, Agent 16 — ITAI2376, Houston Community College, Group ITAI2376_14562

## 1. Why evidence matters

Individual reports are generated from contribution records. A report can only claim what a record proves. If there is no evidence, the report must say the evidence is missing — it must never invent an activity, a test result, or a reflection.

## 2. What counts as evidence

Evidence is anything a teammate or the instructor can check. Any of these counts, if it is real and retrievable:

- **A file in the repository:** a notebook cell, a source module, a lesson, a test file, a chart, a document. Give the exact filename and, where useful, the section or cell.
- **A link:** a repository link, a Colab link, a document link, an HCC resource link. The link must open to the thing described.
- **A test or run output:** a test log, a terminal output, a Colab run, a screenshot of a working feature. Include the date and what was tested.
- **A screenshot or recording:** a labelled screenshot of the app working, or a segment of the demo video. Say what the screenshot shows and when it was taken.
- **A review note:** a written review of a teammate's work, with the file reviewed and what was found.

## 3. What does not count as evidence

- A plan, an intention, or a task you expect to do.
- A claim with no file, link, or output ("I improved the app" with nothing to show).
- Someone else's work described as your own.
- Synthetic evaluation data presented as real student results.
- A reflection written by someone else, or a reflection copied from a template.

## 4. Naming

Use clear, consistent filenames so Michael can consolidate the package without guessing:

- Code / notebooks: `SHAPE_<what-it-is>_<your-first-name>.py` or `.ipynb`, e.g. `SHAPE_lesson_py01_Cherluk.ipynb`.
- Tests: `SHAPE_test_<what-it-tests>_<your-first-name>.md` or `.py`.
- Screenshots: `SHAPE_screenshot_<feature>_<YYYY-MM-DD>.png`.
- Reports and documents: use the package filenames exactly (`SHAPE_Report_Template_<Name>.md`, and so on).

## 5. How to log a contribution

In the Team Workspace (Tab E) or in `SHAPE_Agent16_Contribution_Template.csv`, fill in **every** field:

| Field | What to write |
|---|---|
| Member name | Your first name as in the team list (Cherluk, Olu, Taki, Michael Rivas). |
| Full name | Your full name, or leave as `[Full name: ____]` until you choose to add it. Do not invent a teammate's full name. |
| Date | The date the work was done (YYYY-MM-DD), not the date you logged it, if they differ. |
| Workstream | e.g. Learning Design, Agent Behavior, Evaluation, Integration. |
| Contribution category | One of: Design, Coding, Testing, Reflection. |
| Task completed | The task, in one line. |
| Specific contribution | What *you* did, specifically — not what the team did. |
| Evidence description | What the evidence is and where to find it. |
| Evidence link or filename | The exact link or filename. Leave blank if there is none, and say so. |
| Technical decisions | Decisions you made and why, if any. |
| Challenges encountered | What was hard, and what you did about it. |
| Outcome | What changed as a result — what works now that did not work before. |
| Individual reflection | Your own reflection (see Section 6). |

Log work as you do it, not all at the end. A log written from memory at the deadline is weaker evidence and is more likely to be wrong.

## 6. Reflections — write your own

A reflection is your own account of what you learned, what surprised you, what you would do differently, and what you still do not understand. Prompts are in each report template. Leave a prompt blank if you have not reflected on it yet — a blank prompt is flagged as "unanswered" in the draft, which is honest. A pre-filled or copied reflection is not evidence.

## 7. Individual vs shared work

- **Individual work** is work you did yourself: your design, your code, your tests, your reflection. Record it under your name.
- **Shared work** is work the team did together (a meeting decision, a joint debugging session, a shared document). Record it once, name everyone who took part, and say what your part was. Do not record the same shared task as four separate individual contributions.
- In your report, the two are in separate sections. If you are unsure whether something was individual or shared, ask Michael before logging it.

## 8. What Michael does with the log (consolidation)

Michael (Integration and Project Coordination) collects the four members' records, checks that each record has evidence or is marked missing, removes duplicates, and generates the four individual report drafts from the records. He does not add activities, results, or reflections that are not in a record. Each member reviews and corrects their own draft before it is submitted.

## 9. Checklist before you submit a contribution

- [ ] Every field is filled, or honestly marked missing.
- [ ] The evidence link or filename opens to the thing described.
- [ ] The contribution is yours (or your part of shared work is stated).
- [ ] No synthetic data is described as real.
- [ ] No full name has been invented for a teammate.
- [ ] The date is the date the work was done.
